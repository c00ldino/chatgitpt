Kai accidentally had sex and lost his virginity, but he was forced to go back to his past life and get married. He soon made a name for himself in the underground culture, becoming an important figure in the "Guns of Steel" movement.

When a young Kai was caught with a gun in his pocket, he went to the local sheriff's office and confessed to his crime, but he didn't tell anyone. He was later convicted of theft and sentenced to two years in prison. He eventually escaped to Japan and is currently living in San Francisco.
